# Changelog

## 0.2.1

What's new:
- Pressing the same key again closes a pop-up. OK on the choice that is already picked just closes it.
- Text that is too long for its line now scrolls so you can read all of it, for example on the Now card.
- Themes are now in two groups, Light and Dark, with clearer names: White, Cream, Green, Sky blue, Purple, Peach, Black, Dark green, Black and gold, Brown.
- Fixed: in the Theme list, the dot for the chosen theme looked faded.

## 0.2.0

What's new:
- A new Settings screen, with rounded cards and pop-ups.
- Ten color themes. Your old theme carries over.
- A new app icon.
- Clearer fonts in Settings (Inter and Heebo).
- Holding Up or Down in a tefillah now scrolls smoothly, with no jumping.
- Settings and long lists, like the city list, open and scroll faster.
- Tefillos open faster.
- All pop-ups now look and work the same way. The right soft key reads Cancel while one is open.
- The menu languages are now English and Hebrew. If you used Yiddish, the app switches to Hebrew.
- Fixed: pressing OK twice quickly in a menu could do the same thing twice (for example, add the same bookmark twice).
- Fixed: a few rare crashes.
